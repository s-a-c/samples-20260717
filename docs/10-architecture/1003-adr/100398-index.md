## Glossary

- **ADR**: Architecture Decision Record - a document that captures important architectural decisions along with their context and consequences.
- **Documentation Lifecycle**: The process of creating, maintaining, reviewing, and retiring documentation throughout a project's lifespan.
- **Implementation Readiness**: A measure of how ready the system is for deployment to production environments.
